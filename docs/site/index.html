The problem
Up to 50%

of a hardware product's cost is parts.

Small teams buy them by email: ask three suppliers, take a quote, maybe push back once. Bargain Bot is built for these smaller buyers, and for AI agents that need to buy something.

How it works

Three steps, no haggling on your side

  1. 1

    Hire it like a colleague

    Item, quantity, your limit, your deadline. Your limit stays secret.

  2. 2

    It haggles like a pro buyer

    Opens low, gives ground slowly, quotes rivals' offers, drops the expensive ones, then a final “beat the best price” round.

  3. 3

    Ten checks before any deal

    Price, quantity, delivery, must-haves, signed confirmation.

Suppliers and prices

Where prices come from

Plenty of sources tell you what a part lists for. None of them sells the price a supplier will actually accept. Only negotiating finds that.

Trust

No valid deal, no fee.

Masumi locks the fee in escrow on Cardano before the negotiation starts. Our checker decides whether a result is submitted, and only a submitted result lets us collect the fee after the dispute window. Otherwise the buyer gets it back.

It negotiates, and proves its work before it collects a fee.

✓

Good deal

All checks pass. The result is recorded on Cardano and the fee is released.

✗

Bad deal

A supplier's total doesn't add up. No deal, nothing recorded, nothing collected.
